# Feature Engineering

AlphaForge derives technical features from OHLCV market data. All features are
generated by `FeaturePipeline` in this order: trend, momentum, volatility,
volume, and price action.

## Trend Features

| Output column | Purpose | Formula | Trading intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SMA_20` | Short-term trend baseline | 20-period mean of close | Price above the average can indicate positive short-term trend. |
| `SMA_50` | Medium-term trend baseline | 50-period mean of close | Captures the broader trend and can act as dynamic support or resistance. |
| `EMA_20` | Responsive short-term trend measure | 20-period exponential mean of close | Reacts faster than the SMA to recent price changes. |
| `EMA_50` | Responsive medium-term trend measure | 50-period exponential mean of close | Helps identify sustained direction while weighting recent prices more heavily. |

## Momentum Features

| Output column | Purpose | Formula | Trading intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DAILY_RETURN` | One-period fractional return | `Close.pct_change()` | Measures the immediate price return used by many downstream signals. |
| `PRICE_CHANGE_PCT` | One-period percentage price change | `(Close - Previous Close) / Previous Close * 100` | Shows daily directional movement in percentage terms. |
| `MOMENTUM_10` | Ten-period price momentum | `Close - Close.shift(10)` | Positive values indicate price appreciation over ten periods. |
| `MOMENTUM_20` | Twenty-period price momentum | `Close - Close.shift(20)` | Provides a longer look-back momentum signal. |
| `ROC_12` | Twelve-period rate of change | `(Close - Close.shift(12)) / Close.shift(12) * 100` | Quantifies momentum relative to the earlier price level. |
| `RSI_14` | Relative strength oscillator | `100 - 100 / (1 + Avg Gain(14) / Avg Loss(14))` | High or low readings can flag extended buying or selling pressure. |

## Volatility Features

| Output column | Purpose | Formula | Trading intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TRUE_RANGE` | Intraperiod and gap-aware range | `max(High - Low, abs(High - Previous Close), abs(Low - Previous Close))` | Captures the full price movement relevant to risk. |
| `ATR_14` | Smoothed trading range | 14-period mean of true range | Rising ATR indicates expanding volatility and potentially wider risk limits. |
| `HIST_VOL_20` | Annualized return volatility | `std(log(Close / Previous Close), 20) * sqrt(252)` | Estimates the variability of returns on an annualized basis. |
| `ROLLING_STD_20` | Price dispersion | 20-period standard deviation of close | Identifies periods when prices are spreading away from their mean. |
| `DAILY_RANGE_PCT` | Normalized intraday range | `(High - Low) / Close * 100` | Compares intraday movement across price levels. |

## Volume Features

| Output column | Purpose | Formula | Trading intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `VOLUME_SMA_20` | Typical recent trading activity | 20-period mean of volume | Provides a benchmark for identifying unusual participation. |
| `VOLUME_EMA_20` | Responsive recent activity measure | 20-period exponential mean of volume | Gives more weight to the latest volume changes. |
| `VOLUME_RATIO` | Relative volume | `Volume / VOLUME_SMA_20` | Values above one can confirm strong interest in a price move. |
| `VOLUME_CHANGE_PCT` | One-period volume change | `Volume.pct_change()` | Detects abrupt shifts in market participation. |
| `OBV` | Cumulative volume pressure | Cumulative `sign(Close - Previous Close) * Volume` | Rising OBV can support bullish price action; falling OBV can support bearish action. |

## Price Features

| Output column | Purpose | Formula | Trading intuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GAP_PCT` | Opening gap relative to the prior close | `(Open - Previous Close) / Previous Close * 100` | Gaps can reveal overnight news, sentiment, or discontinuities in supply and demand. |
| `OPEN_CLOSE_PCT` | Intraday open-to-close return | `(Close - Open) / Open * 100` | Indicates whether buyers or sellers controlled the session. |
| `HIGH_LOW_PCT` | Normalized session range | `(High - Low) / Close * 100` | Measures intraday price expansion relative to the closing price. |
| `BODY_SIZE` | Candlestick real-body magnitude | `abs(Close - Open)` | A large body reflects decisive movement during the session. |
| `UPPER_WICK` | Distance from candle body to session high | `High - max(Open, Close)` | A long upper wick can indicate rejection at higher prices. |
| `LOWER_WICK` | Distance from session low to candle body | `min(Open, Close) - Low` | A long lower wick can indicate rejection at lower prices. |
