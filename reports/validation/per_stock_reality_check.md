# Per-Stock Benchmark and Cost Reality Check

## Overview

This report provides a single-stock granular breakdown of the **AlphaForge Production Champion Strategy** versus an **Equal-Weight Buy-and-Hold Benchmark** across each of the 5 liquid NSE equities (`RELIANCE.NS`, `TCS.NS`, `HDFCBANK.NS`, `INFY.NS`, `ICICIBANK.NS`).

Each stock was evaluated independently as a single-asset simulation allocated **100% of a ₹1,00,000 test capital** whenever a signal fired (Mode A single non-overlapping position, 10-day fixed exit) under **realistic 2026 NSE delivery transaction costs** over the identical 23-year period (**August 13, 2003 to August 6, 2026 — 5,694 trading days**).
